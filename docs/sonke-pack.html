Lean Canvas

BlockCopy
ProblemPay for taxi, care, till and SASSA-plus-gig work does not land on the 25th. Stats SA’s QLFS still counts about 3 million informal-sector jobs. Savings monitors keep finding most households cannot cover three months without a payday. Stokvels prove people can save together (~11 million members), but leftover still dies in the week it arrives. FNB already built Easy Zero and Shares Zero. The gap is behavioural: WhatsApp “guaranteed 30%” and mashonisa credit teach people to YOLO the rent. Nothing locks leftover in front of Shares Zero.
SolutionCompound Confidence: leftover-first desk on FNB rails. Features: Easy Zero / Savings / leftover Investments; sit-light-go forecast; 60s ETF lesson; leftover-gated Shares Zero marketplace; scam-aware coach; 12 languages; installable PWA.
UVPThe leftover lock in front of FNB Shares Zero. Life first. Only leftover may buy a basket. If leftover is R0, sitting is the product.
AdvantageThe lock is in the money path, not a blog. Rent cannot become a ticker. Coach refuses hot tips. 12 languages. A judge can miss a gig and watch the desk sit.
Customer18–35 township + metro, irregular R4 000–R12 000 / month (taxi, care, till, delivery, SASSA + gig). Secondary: 36–50 weekly-cash caregivers. Language first: isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Afrikaans. Not Private Clients. Personas: Thabo leftover R180; Nomsa care.
Metrics5 000 active desks in 6 months. ≥60% sit when leftover is R0. ≥70% lesson before first invest. 40% hold ≥1 week of rent in Savings. We do not count trades placed.
ChannelsLive PWA compound-confidence.vercel.app. FNB App of the Year / Easy Zero onboarding. Branch + taxi-rank + care-agency QR. Later WhatsApp wrap. Never a Telegram guru.
Costs5 000-desk pilot / month: Vercel R1 200, OpenAI R3 500, quotes+logs R800, WhatsApp wrap R1 000, language QA R8 000. Total R14 500 / month (R87 000 over 6 months).
RevenueUser pays R0. FNB pays R120 per Easy Zero after the lesson, R280 per first leftover slice, R15 / month per desk that sat or parked leftover. 6-month take: ~R792 500. We do not skim leftover.

SSDLC

PhaseWhat we did
RequirementsLeftover-only invest. Never rent, savings buffer, or mashonisa credit into ETFs. Paper, not live FNB. Coach never hot-tips.
DesignEasy Zero / savings / Shares Zero mapping. Weekly invest cap. OpenAI and quotes server-side. Crypto out of universe.
ImplementationGates in money, holdings, ledger canMove, api/_brain. Tests lock the leftover rule.
VerificationVitest + Testing Library, Python unittest, Thabo walk, PWA, language switch.
ThreatsXSS in localStorage desks, prompt injection, scam OTP harvest, paper vs live confusion, client key leak.
ControlsNo secrets in the SPA. Coach regex skip + length cap. Paper badge. Copy says the buy is not a live FNB order. Service worker skips /api/.
PrivacyDemo tape in the browser. No ID numbers, no live bank tokens, no OTP. OpenAI sees leftover totals, not the password.
OpsVercel env OPENAI_API_KEY. Yahoo public chart. Rollback = previous deploy. Demo desks cannot be wiped.

User journey (Thabo)

  1. Continue as Thabo. Leftover is R180. That is all that may leave Main.
  2. Gig missed this week. The desk sits. Savings still covers rent.
  3. Restore the gig. Do the 60-second lesson. Allocate leftover. Close the week.
  4. Open Shares Zero. Buy a leftover slice of FNB Top 40. Ask the desk if you should sit next week.

Architecture (one paragraph)

Vite + React 19 PWA on Vercel. Python serverless /api/forecast, /api/coach, /api/wrap, /api/market. Leftover brain is shared. OpenAI gpt-4o-mini stays server-side. Marketplace pulls Yahoo JSE ETF charts, caches three minutes, blocks crypto. Desks persist in localStorage. Paper trading on live prices. Not a live FNB order.
