1. What we are building

House Money is not a full stock-trading app. It is a confidence product. It answers one question for someone with gig money, SASSA, a messy payday, or a till: after rent, food, airtime and stokvel are locked, is there leftover — and if there is, what is the grown-up next step?

The rule: rent is not a ticker. If it is not leftover, it is not a position. If leftover is R0, you sit. Sitting is the feature, not a failure.

The on-ramp is FNB, not Telegram: Easy Zero (R0 a month), eWallet (cash on a number), Shares Zero (0% brokerage on FNB ETFs, no minimum). Those rails are mocked in the demo. There is no live bank API. Paper money, real rules.

2. The goal

Win the inclusion track by showing a complete, usable app a judge can walk in five minutes:

  • A person with irregular income can see this week's leftover.
  • They cannot invest rent, groceries, or mashonisa credit.
  • They learn what an ETF is before they size a paper buy.
  • A WhatsApp “guaranteed 30%” tip is rug-pulled.
  • When leftover is real, the exit is Easy Zero → eWallet → Shares Zero — not a guru.

If a judge remembers one clip, it should be this: try to fund a trade with borrowed money or rent, and the desk says sit down.

4. The product loop

  1. The desk — this week's tape: inflows, locked bills, leftover, confidence meter.
  2. Allocate — park in eWallet, stack savings, paper an ETF. Cannot assign more than leftover. Keep at least R50 dry before a paper buy.
  3. Learn — five slides: leftover first, ETF basket, zero vig, rugs, FNB rails. No shortcut. “I understand — leftover only” unlocks on the last slide.
  4. Paper buy — Shares Zero ticket for FNB Top 40 (ETFT40). Confirm a paper fill. Fake next-week P&L. Mashonisa leverage is a hard no.
  5. Rug check — tap group-chat tips. Guaranteed returns, OTP harvests, and “borrow to invest” are blocked. A real FNB product note passes.
  6. The pipes — graduate leftover: open Easy Zero, park eWallet, then open Shares Zero, in that order.

Confidence lights up only when three things are true: lesson done, R50 dry powder, and at least one rug catch. Then: you may size. Lightly.

5. Why this concept

South African inclusion is not “download a brokerage.” Payday is irregular. Mashonisa credit is close. WhatsApp tips are closer. A stock chart without a leftover gate teaches people to YOLO the rent.

House Money takes the DNA of a trader app (paper ticket, ETF, P&L) and puts a lock on it: life first, leftover second, FNB third, ego never. That is money confidence for the track FNB is actually judging — not another crypto inbox.

Languages: all 12 official languages. Eleven written packs, plus captioned SASL (sign is visual; we did not fake a written sign language).
