Problem statement (evidence)

Pay for taxi, care, till and SASSA-plus-gig work does not land on the 25th. Stats SA’s QLFS still counts about 3 million informal-sector jobs. Industry savings monitors keep finding that most households cannot cover three months without a payday. Stokvels prove people can save together (NASASA: ~11 million members), but leftover still dies in the week it arrives.

FNB already built Easy Zero and Shares Zero (R0 fee, 0% on FNB ETFs, no minimum). The gap is behavioural: WhatsApp “guaranteed 30%” and mashonisa credit teach people to YOLO the rent. FSCA keeps warning about those tips. Nothing sits in front of Shares Zero and says: life first, leftover only, sit if leftover is R0.
